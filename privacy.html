Jeremy Makes Music Social Publisher — Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026 · Last updated 23 October 2026

This policy explains how the private Jeremy Makes Music Social Publisher processes information when its owner connects a TikTok account.

Information processed

The application may process the authorised TikTok account identifier and profile details, OAuth access and refresh credentials, draft text, media, publishing choices, post identifiers, status and diagnostic records.

Purpose

Information is used only to authenticate the authorised account, prepare owner-supplied content, show review and approval controls, carry out explicitly confirmed publishing actions, prevent duplicates, and record results for recovery and audit.

Storage and security

Operational data is kept in the owner’s private local automation environment. Credentials are stored through the automation platform’s protected credential store and are not intentionally displayed in public pages or logs.

Sharing

Relevant content and account instructions are sent to TikTok only when needed to provide the requested integration. Information is not sold and is not used for third-party advertising.

Retention and control

Records are retained only while useful for operation, audit or recovery and may be deleted by the owner. TikTok access can be revoked through the connected TikTok account or by removing the application credential.

Google account data

The Hermes assistant, which runs privately on my own Mac, connects to my own Google account through Google sign-in. It only works on my own account and doesn’t access anyone else’s.

  • Gmail: it reads messages and creates drafts. It can’t send email; I send everything myself.
  • Calendar: it reads events and adds or changes them only after I approve each change.
  • Drive, Docs and Sheets: it reads files and edits or creates documents and sheets only after I approve.

The sign-in token is stored only on my Mac. Google data is used only to help me manage my own email, calendar and files. It isn’t sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models, and nobody else ever sees it.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time from your Google account permissions. For questions, contact me through jeremymakesmusic on Gumroad.

Children

This private application is not directed to children.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be directed to Jeremy Makes Music through its official social-media account.
