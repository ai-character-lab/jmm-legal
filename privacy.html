Jeremy Makes Music — Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026 · Last updated 9 November 2026

This policy explains how the private Jeremy Makes Music Social Publisher and OpenCode's private Google-account integration process information when Jeremy connects TikTok or Google accounts.

Information processed

The Social Publisher may process the authorised TikTok account identifier and profile details, OAuth access and refresh credentials, draft text, media, publishing choices, post identifiers, status and diagnostic records. When enabled and authorized, OpenCode's Google connectors may process Drive files, Gmail messages, Calendar events, and YouTube video, comment and caption information for tasks Jeremy requests.

Purpose

Information is used only to authenticate the authorised account, prepare owner-supplied content, show review and approval controls, carry out explicitly confirmed actions, prevent duplicates, and record results for recovery and audit. OpenCode's Google connectors are request-driven and do not scan accounts in the background.

Storage and security

Operational data is kept in the owner's private local automation environment. OAuth refresh tokens are stored on Jeremy's Mac in access-restricted local files, not in OpenCode configuration or logs. Credentials are not intentionally displayed in public pages or logs.

Sharing and AI-assisted processing

Relevant content and account instructions are sent to TikTok only when needed to provide the requested integration. When Jeremy asks OpenCode to process Google data, relevant content may be included in the request to the AI model provider selected for that OpenCode session. The provider processes it to generate the requested result under its own service terms and data-handling practices. OpenAI states that API content is not used to train models by default unless the account opts in; abuse-monitoring logs may contain prompts and responses and are retained for up to 30 days by default, subject to exceptions. Other providers may have different practices.

Information is not sold or used for third-party advertising. OpenCode is configured to require explicit approval for email sends and permanent deletions, Calendar changes and sharing, and YouTube uploads, publication and deletions.

Retention and control

Records are retained only while useful for operation, audit or recovery and may be deleted by the owner. TikTok and Google access can be revoked through the connected account's permissions page or by removing the local application credential.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. OpenAI's data controls are described in its API data documentation. For questions, contact Jeremy Makes Music through jeremymakesmusic on Gumroad.

Children

This private application is not directed to children.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be directed to Jeremy Makes Music through its official social-media account.
